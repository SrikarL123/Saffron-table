More Than Just a Meal

The Saffron Table began in 2014 as a family kitchen with one idea: slow-cooked Indian food, served warmly. Hand-ground masalas, dum biryani sealed and cooked to order, and a dining room built for long, easy conversations.

Whether it is a weeknight dinner or a birthday for twenty, we keep the same promise: fresh ingredients, honest portions and a table that is always ready.
